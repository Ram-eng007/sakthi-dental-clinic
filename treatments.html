OUR SERVICES

Dental Treatments

Comprehensive dental solutions for every stage of your smile journey.

01. Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Professional cleaning helps remove plaque, tartar and surface stains while supporting healthy gums.

02. Tooth Filling

Tooth-coloured fillings can be used to restore teeth affected by cavities and minor damage.

03. Tooth Extraction

Tooth extraction may be recommended when a tooth cannot be safely restored or when removal is required.

04. Artificial Complete Denture

Complete dentures are designed to replace missing teeth and restore appearance and oral function.

05. Dental Implants

Dental implants provide a stable option for replacing missing teeth.

06. Laser Dentistry

Laser technology can be used for precise dental procedures and selected gum treatments.

07. Root Canal Therapy

Root canal treatment is performed to treat infection or inflammation inside an affected tooth.

08. Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Wisdom teeth may need removal when they cause pain, infection, crowding or other dental problems.

09. Fixed Partial Denture (Bridge)

Dental bridges replace one or more missing teeth using a fixed dental restoration.

10. Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)

Professional whitening can help brighten the appearance of teeth and improve the smile.

11. Veneers

Veneers are thin dental coverings that can improve the appearance of selected teeth.

12. Pediatric Dentistry

Dental care for children focuses on healthy development, prevention and comfortable treatment.

13. Flap Surgery

Periodontal flap procedures may be used to access and treat areas affected by gum disease.

14. Orthodontic Braces

Braces help gradually move teeth into improved alignment and correct selected bite problems.

15. Clear Aligners

Clear aligners offer a discreet approach to gradually improving tooth alignment.
